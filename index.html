Installation guide

How to install LISA on Android

LISA is installed directly from this website instead of Google Play, so Android asks for a few quick approvals. It takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Download LISA

    Tap Download LISA on this page. If your browser says “This type of file can harm your device”, tap Download anyway or Keep. Android shows this message for every APK file, which is simply the format Android apps come in.

  2. 2

    Open the downloaded file

    When the download finishes, tap Open in the notification or at the bottom of your browser. Can’t find it? Open the Files app, go to Downloads and tap LISA.apk.

  3. 3

    Allow the installation (one time)

    Android will say “For your security, your phone isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source.” Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then press the back button.

    Didn’t see this message? Open Settings, search for “Install unknown apps”, choose the app you downloaded with (usually Chrome or Files) and switch on Allow from this source.

  4. 4

    Tap Install

    Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning such as “App blocked” or “Unrecognized app”, tap More details and then Install anyway. This is the standard message for any app that doesn’t come from Google Play.

  5. 5

    Open LISA and allow access

    Tap Open. When LISA asks for permission to access your music and audio files and to show notifications, tap Allow so it can find your songs and show playback controls.

  6. 6

    Switch the permission back off (recommended)

    Once LISA is installed, you can undo step 3 to keep your phone locked down. Go to Settings, search for “Install unknown apps”, select the same app as before and switch Allow from this source off. LISA keeps working normally.

Having trouble?

Why does Android show these warnings?

Android warns about every app that is installed from outside Google Play. It doesn’t mean something is wrong with LISA. Always download LISA from this website, and only install apps from sources you trust.

I don’t see the “Allow from this source” option

Open Settings and search for “Install unknown apps”. Pick the app you downloaded LISA with (Chrome, Files, or another browser) and switch the permission on.

Samsung: Settings, then Apps, then the ⋮ menu, then Special access, then Install unknown apps.

Pixel and most other phones: Settings, then Apps, then Special app access, then Install unknown apps.

Android 7 or older: Settings, then Security, then switch on Unknown sources.

Google Play Protect blocked the installation

Tap More details, then Install anyway. If Play Protect offers Scan app, you can scan first and then install. You don’t need to turn Play Protect off. If there is no “Install anyway” option on your phone, email us with your phone model and we’ll help.

I can’t find the downloaded file

Open the Files (or My Files) app and go to Downloads. In Chrome you can also tap the ⋮ menu and then Downloads.

It says “App not installed”

This usually means your storage is full, the download was interrupted, or an older copy of LISA from a different source is already installed. Free up some space, delete the downloaded file and download it again on Wi-Fi. If you already have LISA from another source, uninstall that copy first and try again.

The download is stuck or keeps failing

Use a stable Wi-Fi connection if you can. When the progress reaches 100%, your phone checks the file, so wait a minute. If it stays stuck, cancel it and start the download again.

How do I update LISA later?

Download the newest LISA.apk from this page the same way and tap Install. Android installs it over your current version and keeps your data.
